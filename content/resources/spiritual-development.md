## Overview

Spiritual development is the lifelong process of becoming more like Jesus Christ through the work of the Holy Spirit and obedience to God's Word.

## Topics

- Prayer
- Bible study
- Worship
- Faith
- Holiness
- Spiritual maturity

## Key Scripture

"Grow in the grace and knowledge of our Lord and Saviour Jesus Christ."
— 2 Peter 3:18

## Goal

To equip believers with practical biblical teaching for everyday Christian living.
