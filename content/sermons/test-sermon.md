# Taking the Message of Jesus to All Nations

**Preacher:** Prophet Gerald Yolamu

**Main Scripture:** Matthew 28:19–20

## Introduction

The ministry of JESUS EMBASSY — FOR ALL NATIONS exists to serve the purpose of taking the message of Jesus Christ to all nations. The Gospel is not meant to remain within the walls of one church, one community, or one country. It is the message of salvation, hope, repentance, and new life through Jesus Christ.

Every believer has a responsibility to know the Gospel, live by it, and share it faithfully with others.

## 1. The Great Commission

In Matthew 28:19–20, Jesus commands His disciples to go and make disciples of all nations, baptising them and teaching them to observe His commands.

This instruction reveals that the mission of the Church extends beyond geographical and cultural boundaries. The message of Jesus must reach people wherever they are.

Our responsibility is not merely to gather people for meetings. We must also help people become disciples who know Christ, grow in His Word, and live according to His teachings.

## 2. The Message Must Be Carried

Romans 10:14–15 teaches the importance of preaching so that people can hear and believe.

People need to hear the Gospel. We can share it through preaching, teaching, prayer, personal conversations, testimonies, digital platforms, and acts of Christian love.

Every opportunity to communicate the truth of Christ should be approached with faithfulness, humility, and love.

## 3. Planting Seeds of the Word

In 1 Corinthians 3:6–7, Paul explains that one person may plant and another may water, but God gives the growth.

This reminds us that ministry is not about seeking personal glory. Our responsibility is to sow the Word faithfully, nurture those who receive it, and depend on God for the results.

Some people may respond immediately, while others may need time, teaching, prayer, and encouragement. We should remain faithful without trying to force spiritual growth.

## 4. Our Vision and Responsibility

The vision of JESUS EMBASSY — FOR ALL NATIONS is expressed in these words:

**Taking the message of Jesus to all nations.**

We desire to make Christ known through biblical teaching, sermons, prayer, spiritual development, testimonies, and resources that help people grow in their faith.

As we serve, we must remain grounded in Scripture, committed to Christ, and careful to give God the glory.

## Conclusion

The Great Commission remains a call to every generation of believers. We are called to make disciples, teach the Word, demonstrate the love of Christ, and faithfully share the Gospel.

May JESUS EMBASSY — FOR ALL NATIONS continue to serve this purpose: making Jesus known and helping people grow as His disciples.

**Reflection:** Who can you encourage, pray for, teach, or share the message of Jesus with this week?

**Prayer:** Lord Jesus, help us to remain faithful to Your Word and Your commission. Give us wisdom, love, courage, and humility as we share Your message. May our lives and ministry bring glory to Your name. Amen.
